Why Attacks Fail on Real secp256k1

Our toy curve has group order N = 18, making brute-force instant. Real secp256k1 has order N ≈ 2²⁵⁶ ≈ 1.15 × 10⁷⁷. An exhaustive search would require checking 10⁷⁷ points. Even with BSGS, storing O(√N) = 2¹²⁸ points requires over 10³⁸ bytes of RAM—more storage than all matter in the observable universe.
